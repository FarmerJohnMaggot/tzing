T-Zing privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

T-Zing is a game made by Farmer John Maggot, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me").

What T-Zing collects

Nothing. T-Zing does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best times, unlocked climbers and settings) is saved only on your own device so the game remembers where you got to. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it; you can also clear it with Settings › Reset progress in the game.

Purchases

If you buy anything in T-Zing, the payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. I never see your payment details.

Children

T-Zing collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: farmerjohnmaggot@gmail.com.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
