How to play

  • Leap: drag towards where you want to go, then let go.
  • Stick: tap as an axe touches ice or a crack.
  • Hack: on two axes, keep tapping to work your way up, one axe at a time.
  • Pin: on one axe, tap to place a pin. Fall close to the rock and it stops you; leap far out and you fall all the way.
  • Grapple: in mid-air, tap the wall to fire it.

Common questions

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device. It survives updates; deleting the app removes it.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the game's Settings and tap Restore (it's also on the purchase screen). Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. The full game also works on every device that uses that Apple ID, and with Family Sharing.

There's no sound. T-Zing follows your iPhone's silent switch. Check the switch and Settings › Sound in the game.
