T-Zing support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email farmerjohnmaggot@gmail.com with your iPhone model and the peak you were on. I read every message.

How to play

Hold your finger down while hanging from an axe and you'll swing; let go to fling. At the top of the arc, tap to sink an axe into the rock or ice. With nothing in reach, a tap fires your grapple. Tap to hack up one axe at a time when you want a rest.

Pins

You carry six pins. Double-tap (after a short pause) to place one. A placed pin catches your fall; if you fall a long way, or all the way to the ground, you go back to your last pin (+15 seconds). With no pin in, a fall to the ground restarts the climb. Restock pins at ledge bolts, old pitons and the via ferrata.

Stars

Each peak has three stars: reach the summit, climb with no falls, and beat the time shown on the peak screen.

Daily and weekly

A new daily climb arrives at midnight, and a new weekly peak with a twist every Monday.

Settings

Rock face: left or right, whichever keeps your thumb clear. Aim line: off (default), bold or light. Training replays the training climb. Restore purchase restores the full game on a new device.

Privacy

T-Zing collects no data. Your progress is saved only on your device.

Common questions

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device. It survives updates; deleting the app removes it.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the game's Settings and tap Restore (it's also on the purchase screen). Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. The full game also works on every device that uses that Apple ID, and with Family Sharing.

There's no sound. T-Zing follows your iPhone's silent switch. Check the switch and Settings › Sound in the game.
